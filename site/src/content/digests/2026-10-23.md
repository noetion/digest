OpenAI is pursuing a new $30 billion private round that would push its pre-money valuation above $1 trillion, inDrive signed MOUs with Pony.ai and Nvidia to begin robotaxi tests in Astana in early 2027, and Amazon and national agencies warn that the PHP2Shell vulnerabilities exposed by AI tools are being broadly exploited. The thread: big private capital and real-world deployment plans are moving quickly while AI-assisted vulnerability discovery is creating an urgent operational security problem for web infrastructure.

## OpenAI is reportedly seeking up to $30 billion in private funding at a $1.25 trillion pre-money valuation

- **What happened:** TechCrunch reports OpenAI is close to securing a private raise of up to $30 billion at a $1.25 trillion pre-money valuation, led by Coatue and investors in the UAE, with terms still under negotiation and OpenAI declining comment.
- **Why it matters:** A private round at this scale would set a new record for private-company valuation and raise the valuation bar ahead of OpenAI's confidential IPO filing; it alters the baseline for public-market pricing and comparable private rounds such as Anthropic's recent fundraises.
- **Outlook:** Anthropic’s expected S-1 filing, reported as imminent by the same outlets, and any confirmed close of OpenAI’s $30 billion round will be the next concrete market signals to watch.

## inDrive, Pony.ai, and Nvidia sign MOU to begin robotaxi tests in Astana in first half of 2027

- **What happened:** inDrive said it signed a memorandum of understanding with Pony.ai and Nvidia to explore deploying Pony.ai Gen-7 robotaxis on inDrive’s platform, with testing slated to start in Astana in the first half of 2027 using safety operators initially and driverless rides as the eventual goal.
- **Why it matters:** The plan pairs Pony.ai’s Gen-7 vehicles with Nvidia’s Hyperion stack and Halos safety system, signaling a platform approach that outsources vehicle hardware, perception and compute to specialist vendors and could lower up-front integration costs for operators in emerging markets.
- **Outlook:** The start of supervised testing in Astana in the first half of 2027 is the next concrete milestone the companies set for this collaboration.

<small>Sources: [techcrunch.com](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/uber-rival-indrive-eyes-2027-robotaxi-tests-in-central-asia-with-pony-ai-and-nvidia/)</small>

## Amazon and national agencies say PHP2Shell vulnerabilities discovered with AI are being broadly exploited

- **What happened:** Ars Technica reports Amazon warned it saw an exploitation attempt four days before the public disclosure of the PHP2Shell vulnerabilities, and that the flaws identified with help from OpenAI and Google models are now being broadly exploited in the wild; PHP maintainers released patches the same day.
- **Why it matters:** The incident shows AI-assisted discovery of long-standing language vulnerabilities can produce rapid, large-scale exploitation windows, forcing immediate patching or takedowns and creating operational risk for any internet-facing PHP deployments.
- **Outlook:** The immediate next milestone is organizations applying the PHP maintainer updates released the same day; national advisories and further exploit telemetry from Amazon, the NSA, or UK authorities will be the public indicators of patch uptake and residual exploitation.
