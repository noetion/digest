Today’s stories converge on agents: OpenAI enabled agent-driven checkout with a limited ChatGPT Wallet test; LangChain raised $300 million to manage agents for enterprises; Meituan published LongCat-2.5, a 1.6T-parameter agent model with Huawei-only deployments; an open-source DeepSeek V3 JAX demo shows compact, reproducible agent architectures; and MathArena named Gemini 4 Carbon the new math leader while exposing big cost differences.

## OpenAI launches ChatGPT Wallet to let its dots agents shop and complete checkout for limited U.S. Pro users

- **What happened:** OpenAI rolled out ChatGPT Wallet to a limited set of U.S. ChatGPT Pro subscribers 18+, letting dots agents propose items and complete purchases using tokenized payment cards after the user reviews and approves via biometric security. Merchants receive shipping and contact details from the dot and card tokens are used so the agent and merchant never see raw card numbers.
- **Why it matters:** Engineers building agent flows and integrations now have a concrete, user-facing example of agent-initiated payments and tokenized card flows, including a merchant-facing Commerce Partner Playbook OpenAI published to help sites support agent shoppers.
- **Outlook:** OpenAI plans to add user controls such as spending limits and category/time pre-approval and to expand retailer support beyond the initial limited launch, per the company’s product notes.

## DeepSeek V3 published as a 190-line pure JAX demo with checkpoints and a MacBook Air training run

- **What happened:** The DeepSeek-V3 implementation and v1.0 checkpoint were released as a 190-line pure JAX repo, including training logs, a 138 MB checkpoint, full scripts, tests, and instructions that reproduce a 12,000-step run on a 24 GB MacBook Air M5 and an ROCm container run that reaches similar validation losses.
- **Why it matters:** Engineers get a compact, readable implementation of MLA, decoupled RoPE, Dense MoE routing, and multi-token prediction that reproduces training metrics and provides pinned requirements and test suites for reproduction and ablation experiments.
- **Outlook:** The repository’s v1.0 release artifacts, ROCm container, and published metrics (metrics-main.jsonl and related logs) are the concrete artifacts the community can reproduce immediately to verify the reported 12k-step results and ROCm performance.

<small>Sources: [github.com](https://github.com/Vaibhaav-Tiwari/deepseek-v3-jax)</small>

## Meituan publishes LongCat-2.5 weights, a 1.6T-parameter agent model quantized for Chinese chips and self-hosted on 194 Huawei accelerators

- **What happened:** Meituan released LongCat-2.5 weights under the MIT license and a self-hosting guide that shows a serving cluster using 194 Huawei Ascend Atlas A2 accelerators (2 for images, 64 for prompt ingestion, 128 for response generation). The MoE model totals 1.6 trillion parameters with about 48 billion active per token, supports up to one million-token contexts and 128,000-token outputs, and uses 4-bit quantization for experts and activations.
- **Why it matters:** The release provides engineers a large open-weight agent model optimized for non-Nvidia hardware, a self-hosting recipe for a specified Huawei cluster, and benchmark numbers (Terminal Bench v2.1, DeepSWE, BrowseComp) to compare multimodal agent capabilities and scaling choices.
- **Outlook:** The published weights, MIT license, self-hosting guide, and Meituan-run benchmark scores on Terminal Bench v2.1 and DeepSWE are the immediate artifacts for third parties to reproduce or test against in their own evaluations.

<small>Sources: [the-decoder.com](https://the-decoder.com/meituan-releases-a-1-6-trillion-parameter-agent-model-that-never-touched-an-nvidia-gpu/)</small>

## Gemini 4 Carbon tops MathArena’s math leaderboard but costs roughly 15 times more per test than GPT-6.1 Sol in the published runs

- **What happened:** MathArena’s published runs place Google’s Gemini 4 Carbon first on the benchmark with an estimated 85.4% ±3.0% score using reasoning settings that also produced high inference costs; the reported per-run cost for Carbon was about $15.70 versus GPT-6.1 Sol at about $1.07 under the same testing settings.
- **Why it matters:** For engineers choosing models for math- and reasoning-heavy workloads, the MathArena results show a trade-off: Carbon yields the top raw math score in the testbed but does so at substantially higher inference cost relative to GPT-6.1 Sol and other models included in the published comparison.
- **Outlook:** MathArena’s published leaderboard and the November 10, 2026 results are the concrete benchmark snapshot to compare future model updates and cost-performance changes between providers.

<small>Sources: [the-decoder.com](https://the-decoder.com/gemini-4-carbon-takes-the-matharena-crown-but-costs-15-times-more-than-gpt-6-1-sol/)</small>

## LangChain raises $300M Series C at a $3B valuation to position LangSmith as an enterprise agent management platform

- **What happened:** LangChain closed a $300 million Series C led by CapitalG at a $3 billion valuation with participation from GIC, DFJ Growth, IVP, and Sapphire Ventures; the company reports 7,000 LangSmith customers including Nvidia, Toyota, and Salesforce.
- **Why it matters:** The round signals strong investor conviction in vendor-agnostic agent-management tooling and provides LangChain capital to expand LangSmith’s agent-behavior and enterprise features while continuing to support multiple model and cloud backends.
- **Outlook:** LangChain’s reported baseline of 7,000 LangSmith customers is the concrete customer-count signal to watch in future company updates and investor communications.
