OpenAI's cofounder pitched an agent-first future where users no longer learn software, while a large longitudinal study quantifies how externalizing work to AI can erode real learning over two years. Both items matter for engineers building agent runtimes, integrations, and evaluation protocols.

## OpenAI cofounder says future should have 'almost no interface' and persistent context-aware agents

- **What happened:** Greg Brockman said plugins failed because models were not ready and described a goal where ChatGPT becomes an invisible, persistent, context-aware agent that acts autonomously rather than a feature-packed app.
- **Why it matters:** Engineers building agent runtimes and integrations should expect a push toward persistent agents and less user-facing UI, which emphasizes reliable long-running context, action execution, and on-site integration work rather than plugin ecosystems that depend on brittle model behavior.
- **Outlook:** Watch for further product and engineering work from OpenAI and its rivals that shifts investment from plugin-style extensions to persistent agent infrastructure and deployment support.

## 26,000-student study finds AI use raises homework scores but reduces exam performance by up to 24 percent after two years

- **What happened:** Researchers analyzed 30 months of data from over 26,000 grades 7–12 students; six months after first AI use homework scores increased 18 percent and time per assignment fell, while monthly closed-book exam scores fell about 20 percent and entrance exam declines of 18–24 percent emerged after roughly two years.
- **Why it matters:** For engineers building education tools and assessment systems, the study shows AI can replace the practice that produces durable knowledge; metrics like homework completion time and proctored in-person testing will be necessary to detect outsourcing and preserve learning signals.
- **Outlook:** Expect calls for instrumentation that tracks time-on-task, more weight on in-class or proctored assessments, and features that encourage active learning rather than answer generation.
